# Seller statistic for the video (#9)

## Primary stat (problem framing, video 0:00–0:20)

> Nearly 30% of merchants selling digital goods or services report chargeback
> rates of 1% or higher, compared to 21% of merchants selling only physical goods.

- Source: Chargebacks911, *The State of Chargebacks* (survey of 1,000+ merchants)
- Link: https://ad.chargebacks911.com/hubfs/whitepaper-state-of-chargebacks-Chargebacks911.pdf
- Suggested line: "Almost a third of digital-goods sellers run chargeback rates
  above 1% — worse than physical-goods sellers — because a download has no
  tracking number."

## Backup stat (solution framing, ~1:50)

> Digital-goods merchants achieve the highest dispute win rates (~72.6%) —
> because they can show delivery evidence and IP/device logs.

- Source: Chargebacks911, *State of Chargebacks 2025* (as cited by
  winningchargebacks.com/statistics.html — verify against the original report
  before recording)
- Point: ProofVault generates exactly that evidence automatically.
